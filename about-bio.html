My story

A boutique practice, built around you

I founded Australis on a simple belief: a property settlement should never feel like a ticket in a queue. After many years guiding Victorian buyers, sellers and investors through their transactions, I'd seen first-hand how often people are left chasing updates, decoding jargon and bracing for surprise fees.

So Australis does it differently. When you engage me, you work with me — the same trusted hands reading your contract, running your searches and standing on your settlement. No call centres, no case numbers, no hand-offs between strangers. Just one experienced conveyancer who knows your file inside out and explains every step in plain English.

Whether it's a first home in the suburbs, an off-the-plan apartment or a commercial purchase in the CBD, my promise is the same: I'll sweat the detail and the timeline so you can get on with the exciting part — your next chapter.
