PEXA settlements: why electronic is now the norm

RV Ravinder Pal Kaur Virk 7 May 2026 4 min read

Not long ago, settlement meant representatives gathering in a room to swap cheques and paper titles. Today, almost every Victorian settlement happens online. Here's what that means for you.

Electronic settlement through PEXA — the property exchange platform — is now the standard way property changes hands in Victoria. For buyers and sellers, the mechanics happen behind the scenes, but the benefits are real.

What actually changes

Instead of a physical meeting, your conveyancer, the other party's representative and the banks all connect in a secure online workspace. Funds move electronically and the title transfer is lodged at the same time, so everything completes in one coordinated step.

Why it's usually smoother

  • Faster transfer of title. Lodgement happens at settlement, so ownership updates quickly rather than waiting on paperwork.
  • Funds arrive electronically. No bank cheques to bank and clear — your proceeds reach you sooner.
  • Fewer manual errors. Figures are checked digitally by all parties before the button is pressed.
  • No need to attend. You don't have to be anywhere; your conveyancer handles the workspace on your behalf.
“Electronic settlement removes most of the old day-of-settlement drama. The work that makes it smooth still happens in the weeks before — quietly, and on time.”

What still matters

Going digital doesn't remove the need for careful preparation. Identity verification, accurate figures, bank readiness and the right documents still have to be in place ahead of time. That's where your conveyancer earns their keep — so the settlement itself is a non-event.
