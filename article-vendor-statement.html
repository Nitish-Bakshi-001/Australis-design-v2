Selling in 2026? Prepare your vendor statement early

RV Ravinder Pal Kaur Virk 21 May 2026 5 min read

In Victoria you can't advertise a property for sale until the vendor's statement is ready. Leave it to the last minute and you'll hold up your own campaign. Start early and selling becomes the easy part.

Before a property can legally go to market, the seller must prepare a Section 32 vendor's statement. It pulls together everything a buyer is entitled to know — and assembling it takes time, because much of the information comes from third parties.

What goes into it

A compliant statement draws on several sources, and each has its own turnaround:

  • A current title search and plan;
  • Council and water rates, and land-tax information;
  • For units and apartments, owners-corporation certificates and records;
  • Details of any easements, covenants, zoning and overlays;
  • Any notices or orders affecting the property.

Why early matters

Certificates can take days or weeks to come back. If you wait until your agent is ready to launch, you risk missing the photography slot, the first open, or a strong early offer. Preparing the statement while you're still styling the home means you're ready to list the moment you decide.

“The sellers who have the smoothest campaigns are the ones whose paperwork was finished before the first photo was taken.”

Getting it right

An incomplete or inaccurate statement isn't just a delay — it can give a buyer grounds to end the contract later. We prepare your Section 32 carefully, coordinate directly with your agent, and make sure it's both compliant and complete before anything goes live.
