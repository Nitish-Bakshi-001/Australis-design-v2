Meet your conveyancer

You'll work with Ravinder Pal Kaur Virk — not a queue

Australis is run by Ravinder Pal Kaur Virk, a licensed Victorian conveyancer with extensive experience guiding buyers, sellers and investors through settlement. She started the practice because too many people were treated like a file number on the biggest purchase of their lives.

So she built the opposite. Every contract is read by Ravinder Pal Kaur Virk personally, line by line — the legal detail translated into plain English, the risks flagged, and a single point of contact from the first call to the day the keys change hands.

Property is personal. My job is to take the legal weight off your shoulders, tell you the truth in language that makes sense, and make settlement day the calmest part of your move.
